# Architecture Decision Records

Every structural decision is recorded here, on the same plan: **context**, **decision**, **consequences**, **alternatives rejected**. It is the last heading that gives an ADR its value — it says what was considered and then rejected, and why, which no reading of the code can reconstruct.

## How to read this folder

It is not meant to be read end to end. Three uses:

* **Understanding an external brick** — 0001 to 0005 (Rust, Slint, SQLite, LibRaw, Lensfun/LittleCMS).
* **Understanding the data model** — 0007 (a Git-inspired model), 0008 (the version as the unit), 0010 (relative paths).
* **Understanding the rendering** — 0012 (parallelism), 0028 then **0042** and **0043** (render versioning), and the pixel-feature ADRs 0013, 0016–0018, 0029–0035.

## Editing rule

**Before the first public release, an ADR stays freely editable**: an ADR that no longer matches the implementation is corrected in place, not superseded. That latitude stops on the day the project opens to the world — accepted decisions then freeze, and are replaced by new ADRs referencing them, never edited.

What never relaxes on either side of that line: **decide and document before coding**.

### The paths cited by the ADRs before 0042

The ADRs earlier than [0042](0042-versioned-stage-pipeline.md) name `processN.rs` modules — `process1.rs` to `process11.rs` — that **no longer exist**. The pipeline was reorganized into versioned stages ([0042](0042-versioned-stage-pipeline.md)) and then its pre-release history collapsed ([0043](0043-collapse-prerelease-render-history.md)): an operator that lived in a whole copy of the pipeline now lives in `crates/leyline-engine/src/stages/<operator>/vN.rs`.

Those citations are **not** corrected, and that is deliberate: they describe an act at its date — "`process6.rs`, a whole copy of `process5.rs`" tells how the module was created, and rewriting it as a path of today would make the sentence false while making it clickable. The editing rule above governs what an ADR *asserts about the present*, not what it recounts of the past.

To find the code for such an ADR: the operator it describes, under `stages/`, at the version it created.

A superseded ADR is never deleted: it stays readable, with its reasoning of the time. [ADR 0028](0028-process-version-per-feature.md) is the example — its reasoning was correct for the data it had, and it had itself foreseen its reopening.

| [0109](0109-reshape-stage.md) | **Reshape: moving content without inventing any**: every stage changes what a pixel *is*; none changes where it **is**, except the three that recompose the whole frame at once. Nothing could move part of a photograph relative to the rest — a face stretched at a wide-angle edge, a horizon bowed by a correction that fixed the centre, a silhouette a photographer wants eased. Unlike a denoise it needs no model, no weights and no second process: it is geometry, so it belongs in the free engine as a stage, dosable and reversible. A **new stage** (so §5.1 and the golden manifest are untouched for every revision already written — [0103](0103-red-eye-correction.md)'s measured lesson) at **rank 25**, and the rank is the decision: after `lens` 20, before `spot_removal` 30, so that **everything a person places afterwards is placed on what they see** — a spot on a reshaped cheek would otherwise land on unreshaped pixels — because it states something about the *subject* rather than the composition, and because resampling before `sharpen` 190 means what gets sharpened is the final geometry (`rotate` 200 has the opposite arrangement and has to). A `Vec<ReshapePoint>` — `from`, `to`, `radius`, `strength` — with an empty list neutral and no neutral entry, the convention `spot_removal`, `red_eye` and `local_adjustments` already share; coordinates are [0026](0026-mask-spot-coordinate-referential.md)'s post-rotation pre-crop frame, mapped by the helper those two already call. The load-bearing choice is that the stage is defined by its **inverse map** — sample at `p + Σ wᵢ(p)·strengthᵢ·(fromᵢ − toᵢ)`, weight centred on `to` — so at `p = to` the sample lands exactly on `from` *by construction*, and no forward field ever has to be numerically inverted: `rotate::v1`'s technique with a different source formula, ADR 0026's backward remapping family gaining its fourth consumer. Three costs stated rather than discovered: overlapping handles **add** and a large enough sum folds the map (visible in the loupe, not prevented — solving injectivity per drag is not worth it); the canvas keeps its size and the frame edge is **extended** rather than filled with black; and **nothing is invented**, every output pixel comes from an input pixel, which is why this needs no model at all.
